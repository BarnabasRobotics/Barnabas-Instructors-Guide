### Making the Servo Move When You Win

Now you'll connect your game's win logic to physical movement. When the player hits the winning light, your program will command the servo to move—bringing together input, decision-making, and output in one interactive system.

### What You'll Learn

- Control a servo motor with code
- Trigger servo movement from the game's winning condition
- Combine lights, button input, logic, and motion in one program

### Follow Along

1. Follow the video to add servo control to your existing Cyclone program.
2. Play the game and confirm that the servo moves when the winning condition is reached.
3. Check that the servo returns or behaves as expected before playing another round.

### Check Your Work

Before moving on, make sure your project behaves like the example shown in the video. If something isn't working, compare your wiring and code carefully with the video before changing multiple things at once.
