### What You'll Learn

- Identify the LED as an output in the Magic Box system
- Connect the first LED as demonstrated in the video
- Check your wiring before moving on to coding

### Follow Along

1. Follow the wiring shown in the video to connect the first LED.
2. Compare your circuit carefully with the example before powering or programming it.
3. Make sure each connection is secure.

### Check Your Work

Before moving on, make sure your project behaves like the example shown in the video. If something isn't working, compare your wiring and code carefully with the video before changing multiple things at once.
